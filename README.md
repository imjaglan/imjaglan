### About

I'm a software engineer who lives at the seam where **LLM plumbing meets real product surfaces** — auth flows, billing, integrations, the unglamorous stuff that makes AI features actually ship.

Currently a Working Student SWE at **[Symbiofy.ai](https://symbiofy.ai)** while finishing my **Master's in Computer Science** in Germany. Previously built and scaled the frontend of an AI platform serving **15k users** at Savasana.

I care about fast feedback loops, type-safe contracts end-to-end, and code that future-me won't have to apologize for.
